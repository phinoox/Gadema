# 🎨 User Experience & ADHD Workflow

---

## 🧠 The Core Objective

> **Reduce friction between thought and capture.**

The Anima workflow is designed to match the non-linear, associative nature of the ADHD brain. Our goal is to eliminate "Decision Fatigue"—the mental exhaustion that comes from being forced to organize your thoughts before you've even finished thinking them.

---

## 🌊 Principle 1: Universal Manifestation

**Your ideas can manifest as anything.**

Anima does not restrict your creativity to specific silos. Whether you are defining a character, mapping a location, or designing a game mechanic, you use the same consistent set of tools. Every concept in Anima—regardless of what it "is"—shares the same powerful capabilities:
* **Universal Tagging**: Categorize any idea instantly.
* **Media Attachment**: Add images, audio, or files to any concept.
* **Versioned History**: Track the evolution of any idea through snapshots.
* **Bi-directional Linking**: Connect any two ideas in your world.

The system adapts to your creation, ensuring that as your world grows in complexity, your toolkit remains simple and familiar.

---

## 🛠️ Principle 2: The Rituals of Progress

To maintain momentum, we separate **Safety** from **Commitment**. This prevents the anxiety of "losing work" while providing a satisfying sense of achievement.

### 1. The Safety Net (Autosave)
* **The Action**: Happens silently in the background.
* **The Goal**: Purely for recovery. If you crash or close the tab, your work is safe. 
* **The UX**: It does not interrupt you and stays invisible so that it doesn't break your "Flow."

### 2. The Commitment Ritual (Snapshot Save)
* **The Action**: An intentional act of saving a version.
* **The Goal**: To mark a milestone. This turns a moment of work into a permanent part of your history.
* **The UX**: Requires a tiny, one-line comment. This micro-reflection helps transition the brain from "Active Creation" to "Review/Rest."

---

## 🧭 Principle 3: Interaction Patterns (Navigating the Flow)

Our UI patterns are designed to manage your focus and provide "scaffolding" only when you ask for it.

### 🔍 Focus vs. Context (The Split Workspace)
* **Focus Mode**: A clean, distraction-free area for writing/creating.
* **Contextual Preview**: Small, collapsible "ghost" windows that show related elements without forcing you to switch screens. You can see the connection without leaving your task.

### 🕸️ The Visual Graph (Connections over Lists)
* Instead of rigid folders and nested lists, Anima uses **Connection Threads**.
* If you have an idea in Scene A that relates to a character in Scene B, you simply drag a thread between them. This mimics how the brain actually associates ideas—through links, not hierarchies.

### ⚡ The "Where To Go Next?" Panel (Decision Support)
* Decision paralysis is a major hurdle. After a **Snapshot Save**, Anima gently offers suggestions:
    * *Quick Wins*: Low-energy tasks to keep momentum (e.g., "Add a tag").
    * *Next Steps*: Logical progressions for your narrative.
* These are always dismissible. They are suggestions, not commands.

---

## 🔄 The Complete Loop: Flow $\rightarrow$ Commit $\rightarrow$ Decide

This is the rhythmic cycle of working with Anima:

1.  **PHASE: FLOW**: Capture ideas rapidly in a clean environment. Use the **Idea Dump** for unorganized thoughts.
2.  **PHASE: COMMIT**: Perform a **Snapshot Save**. Write your one-line milestone comment.
3.  **PHASE: DECIDE**: Review the **Suggestions Panel**. Choose a quick win to keep momentum, or dismiss it to return to Flow.

---

## 🚫 Anti-Patterns We Avoid

| The Friction Point | Why It Fails ADHD Users | Our Approach |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Mandatory Outlines** | Blocks the ability to start writing. | Outlines are optional, later additions. |
| **Save Prompts** | Creates anxiety and breaks focus. | Silent autosave for safety; intentional snapshots for progress. |
| **Nested Menus** | Causes "lost in the woods" feeling. | Graph-based connections and visible timelines. |
| **Complex Forms** | Kills the "Idea Capture" momentum. | Minimalist, single-click interactions. |
